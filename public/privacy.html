Privacy Policy & Google Play Data Safety

Dulhaniya Technologies Private Limited ("Dulhaniya", "we", "our", or "us") operates the Dulhaniya Android Application and website at dulhaniya.netlify.app. We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy, dignity, and security of our customers and beauty specialist partners.

1. Information We Collect

To provide luxury salon and bridal services at your doorstep, we collect the following minimal information:

  • Contact Information: Full name, telephone number, and optional email address to confirm appointments and dispatch verified specialists.
  • Delivery Address & Location: Doorstep address and city selected by you to ensure the specialist arrives at the correct venue. We only collect location data when you schedule or track an active appointment.
  • Service Preferences: Selected beauty rituals, Bring-Your-Own-Product (BYOP) preferences, and time slot selections.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, schedule, and dispatch certified female beauty specialists to your home.
  • To generate the secret 4-digit doorstep OTP code ensuring services only begin upon your verified arrival.
  • To send real-time appointment status updates via SMS, push notifications, and WhatsApp.
  • To provide emergency 24x7 SOS safety dispatch assistance when requested by customer or specialist.

3. Data Safety & Security Disclosures

  • No Data Selling: We NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal or appointment data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.
  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the Dulhaniya Android App, our servers, and payment gateways are encrypted using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • Data Deletion: You have the right to request deletion of your account and booking history at any time by contacting privacy@dulhaniya.app.

4. Android Permissions Used

  • INTERNET: Required to load beauty catalog, slot availability, and order status.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used by the offline service worker to switch seamlessly between cloud and local modes.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to notify you when your specialist is on the way.
  • VIBRATE: Used for haptic feedback during doorstep arrival and SOS triggers.
